Support

A few boundaries are intentional. Others come from Android itself.

Known limitations

  • No scroll detection. Ergo Reminder intentionally tracks foreground app time only.
  • No browser-tab awareness. Android usage APIs can identify the foreground app, but not reliably which Chrome tab, web page, or in-app content is being viewed.
  • No app-specific content detection. See above; Ergo does not 'see' inside the app, just that it's in the foreground.
  • No cloud sync. The app is intentionally local-first.
  • No schedules, posture camera, or advanced adherence analytics. See here if you need help on how to stretch your neck.
  • OEM battery managers may interrupt monitoring. This is most common on Xiaomi / HyperOS / MIUI, Huawei, Oppo / Realme, Vivo, and some Samsung devices.
  • English only.
  • The 30-second reset window (the default value in the free version) can feel slightly jittery because foreground detection is polled once per second, rather than continuously.

Troubleshooting basics

  • Confirm that the Usage Access permission is still granted.
  • Confirm that the Overlay Permission is still granted.
  • Press the 'Re-check Permissions' button.
  • Confirm the apps you expect to monitor are still selected.
  • On some devices, optional battery optimization exemption can improve reliability.
  • If Android force-stops the app, the monitoring will switch to 'Off'. Open the app and click 'Start Monitoring' to start again.

Purchases & Premium Troubleshooting

Premium Not Appearing After Purchase

Verify that Google Play is signed into the account used to make the purchase, reconnect to the internet, then navigate to Settings > Premium > Restore / Check purchase in the app. Please do not repurchase to troubleshoot an existing transaction.

Pending Payments

Premium features become available automatically after Google Play confirms payment. Reopen the app or re-check the purchase once the payment resolves. A pending, failed, or cancelled transaction will not unlock Premium.

Purchasing Unavailable

If you cannot open the purchase screen or complete checkout, check your internet connection and ensure your Google Play Store app is updated. Product availability depends on your Google Play account and region.

Temporary Verification Failures

If previously confirmed Premium access temporarily fails verification (e.g., due to network drops), your local Premium access is retained.

Unconfirmed Premium Ownership

If ownership cannot be confirmed, check your active purchasing account in Google Play and retry. Your saved Premium settings and local history remain intact (subject to normal 90-day history expiry). Note that this message indicates an entitlement check failure, not necessarily a refund.

Reinstalling or Switching Devices

Eligible ownership can be restored across devices through your Google Play account. However, because Ergo Reminder is local-first, local app settings, history, and streak data are not synced across devices.

Need Further Support?

If issues persist, reach out to support@protectyourneck.us. To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • Your app version and device model
  • The exact action you performed and any error message shown

Privacy & Security Note: Do not include passwords, payment card details, or raw purchase tokens in your email, and do not post order or account identifiers in public forums or issue reports.
