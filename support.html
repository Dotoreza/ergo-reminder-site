Support

A few boundaries are intentional. Others come from Android itself.

Known limitations

  • No scroll detection. Ergo Reminder intentionally tracks foreground app time only.
  • No browser-tab awareness. Android usage APIs can identify the foreground app, but not reliably which Chrome tab, web page, or in-app content is being viewed.
  • No app-specific content detection. Distinguishing YouTube Shorts from ordinary YouTube viewing, or productive browsing from doomscrolling inside the same app, would require brittle, unsupported behavior and is outside the MVP.
  • No cloud sync. The app is intentionally local-first.
  • No schedules, posture camera, or advanced adherence analytics. Those are out of scope for the MVP.
  • OEM battery managers may interrupt monitoring. This is most common on Xiaomi / HyperOS / MIUI, Huawei, Oppo / Realme, Vivo, and some Samsung devices.
  • English only.
  • The 30-second reset window can feel slightly jittery because foreground detection is polled once per second rather than continuously.

Troubleshooting basics

  • Confirm Usage Access is still granted.
  • Confirm overlay permission is still granted.
  • Confirm the apps you expect to monitor are still selected.
  • On some devices, optional battery optimization exemption can improve reliability.
  • If Android force-stops the app, monitoring will stop until the app is opened again.
