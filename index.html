A scroll reminder you don't have to remember.

Screen time break reminders with auto-detection and almost no setup. You choose the apps where you scroll too much. Ergo detects when you're using those apps, and shows a break reminder after 20 minutes of use. That's it. No 'Start' button to press, no schedules to manage, no spying.

What it does

You choose the apps

You choose the apps where you doomscroll too much (TikTok, Instagram, the Weather app... we don't judge)

It tracks time, not behavior

Ergo Reminder auto-magically detects when you start using the apps you've chosen. No schedules to manage and no timers to start every time you scroll.

It reminds without interfering

Take the break, dismiss the reminder, and go back to what you were doing. Ergo doesn't lock you out or tell you how to use your phone.

It just works.

Ergo works on the honor system. The reminder is automatic. Taking the screen break is up to you.
