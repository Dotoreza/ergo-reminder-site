Break the scroll posture before it breaks you.

Ergo Reminder watches foreground time in the apps you choose and gives you a simple, dismissible reminder after 20 accumulated minutes. No cloud sync. No behavioral analytics. No app blocking. Just a nudge to straighten up.

What it does

You choose the apps

Select the Android apps you want monitored. Changing the monitored set resets the current session accumulator.

It tracks time, not behavior

Ergo Reminder does not detect scrolling or inspect app content. It only tracks whether a selected app is in the foreground.

It reminds without interfering

The overlay is informational and dismissible. The app does not block, close, or control third-party apps.

Built to stay simple.

The MVP focuses on reliable foreground-app timing, a clear reset window, and a clean reminder flow. That keeps the product understandable, private, and easier to trust.
