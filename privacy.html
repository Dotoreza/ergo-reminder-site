Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Ergo Reminder ("we", "our", or "us") provides an Android application designed to help users take ergonomic break reminders while using selected apps.

Your privacy matters to us. Ergo Reminder is designed to work locally on your device with minimal data collection.

Summary

  • Ergo Reminder is a local-first app.
  • App usage data stays on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use behavioral analytics.
  • We do not use cloud sync.
  • Crash reporting is disabled by default and is only enabled if you explicitly opt in.

Information We Access

1. App Usage Access

If you grant Usage Access permission, Ergo Reminder can determine which app is currently in the foreground.

This is used only to:

  • detect whether one of your selected monitored apps is active
  • measure accumulated foreground time in those selected apps
  • trigger a reminder overlay after the configured interval

Ergo Reminder does not use this permission to:

  • read the contents of other apps
  • detect scrolling
  • determine which browser tab you are viewing
  • inspect messages, typed text, passwords, or screen content
  • block, close, minimize, or control third-party apps

2. Display Over Other Apps

If you grant overlay permission, Ergo Reminder can display a reminder overlay on top of other apps.

This is used only to show the ergonomic reminder when the threshold is reached.

3. Battery Optimization Exemption

You may optionally grant battery optimization exemption. This is used only to improve monitoring reliability on certain devices.

Granting or denying this option does not change our data practices.

Information Stored On Your Device

Ergo Reminder stores the following information locally on your device:

  • the list of apps you selected for monitoring
  • the accumulated foreground timer state
  • the daily reminder or "bump" count
  • whether battery optimization exemption has been prompted
  • whether monitoring is enabled
  • whether you opted in to crash reporting

This information is stored locally and is not uploaded to us.

Crash Reporting

Crash reporting is disabled by default.

If you explicitly opt in, Ergo Reminder may send crash diagnostics to our crash reporting provider to help us identify and fix app stability problems.

This may include technical information such as:

  • device model
  • Android version
  • app version
  • stack traces
  • technical crash context

Crash reporting is not enabled unless you turn it on.

If you reset app stats or local data, your crash-reporting preference is preserved because it is treated as a separate privacy choice.

What We Do Not Do

Ergo Reminder does not:

  • sell your personal information
  • rent your personal information
  • use advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising
  • use behavioral analytics
  • sync your data to the cloud
  • collect browsing history
  • detect scroll behavior
  • inspect browser tabs or in-app content
  • read messages, contacts, photos, or microphone/camera data
  • block or control third-party apps

Data Sharing

We do not share your app usage data with third parties.

If you opt in to crash reporting, limited technical crash data may be shared with the crash-reporting provider solely for debugging and stability purposes.

Data Retention

Because Ergo Reminder is local-first, most app data remains on your device until you remove it, reset the app's local data, or uninstall the app.

If crash reporting is enabled by you, crash data sent to the crash-reporting provider is retained according to that provider's retention settings.

Your Choices

You can control Ergo Reminder by:

  • choosing which apps to monitor
  • enabling or disabling monitoring
  • granting or revoking Usage Access
  • granting or revoking overlay permission
  • granting or denying battery optimization exemption
  • opting in or out of crash reporting
  • resetting app stats and local data
  • uninstalling the app

Children's Privacy

Ergo Reminder is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use a local-first design to minimize data collection and reduce privacy risk. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International Users

Ergo Reminder is intended for users who choose to install it on their Android devices. If crash reporting is enabled by you, technical crash data may be processed by the crash-reporting provider in accordance with their systems and infrastructure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above.
