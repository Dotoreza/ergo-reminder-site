Features

A small Android tool with a narrow job: watch selected apps and remind you to reset your posture.

Core MVP features

  • User-selected monitored apps
  • 20-minute accumulated foreground-app reminder interval
  • 30-second reset window after leaving monitored apps
  • Dismissible overlay reminder
  • Daily bump count
  • Live timer displayed in mm:ss
  • Local-first settings and stats storage
  • Crash reporting opt-in only

What Ergo Reminder does not do

  • It does not detect scrolling.
  • It does not inspect app contents or browser tabs.
  • It does not block, close, or minimize third-party apps.
  • It does not sync your data to the cloud.
  • It does not use behavioral analytics.
