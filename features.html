Features

An elegant Android app with one job: watch your chosen apps and remind you to reset your posture after 20 minutes.

Core features

  • The user chooses the apps to monitor
  • A 20-minute reminder interval, based on accumulated time as the foreground app
  • When leaving a monitored app, the timer will reset after 30 seconds
  • A dismissible overlay, that reminds you to take a break
  • Daily bump count
  • The live timer displayed in mm:ss
  • All settings and statistics stay on your device
  • Crash reporting is off by default and opt-in only

What Ergo Reminder does not do

  • It does not detect scrolling.
  • It does not inspect app contents or browser tabs.
  • It does not block, close, or minimize third-party apps.
  • It does not sync your data to the cloud.
  • It does not use behavioral analytics.
