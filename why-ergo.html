Why Ergo?

Why build yet another screen break reminder? Because I couldn't find one I liked:

  • Opal: Powerful, but much more focused on limiting and blocking apps than I wanted.
  • one sec: Great if you want friction before or during app use. I wanted something simpler: leave me alone until I really need a break.
  • Work Break / Stand Up!: Built around schedules and timers. I don't want reminders at 10:20 because I told an app I might be using my phone then. I want one after I've actually used it for 20 minutes.
  • Moova / Wakeout: More focused on guided movement and exercises. I don't need a gym, I just need to take a scroll break sometimes.
  • Screen Zen / StayFree: Built largely around limits, budgets and blocking. I'm not trying to stop myself from scrolling, I just don't want to do it for an hour without a break.
  • NoScroll, StopScroll, and others: They're trying to stop doomscrolling. I'm not. I know I'm wasting time. I just want to take a break occasionally.
  • Many others: They expect me to start a timer or set a schedule. If I have to remember to start the reminder, eventually I won't.

So I built Ergo differently.

Pick the apps you scroll too much. Ergo quietly keeps track of how long you're actually using them. After 20 minutes, it reminds you to take a quick break.

No schedule. No daily limit. No Start button. No app blocking.
