FAQ

Short answers to the most common questions about how Ergo Reminder works.

Does Ergo Reminder detect scrolling?

No. It only tracks foreground time in the apps you selected.

Can it see what I am reading or typing?

No. It does not inspect screen contents, browser tabs, messages, or typed text.

Does it block apps or force breaks?

No. The reminder overlay is informational and dismissible.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Ergo Reminder is local-first and does not use cloud sync.

Why does the timer sometimes reset a little late?

The app polls foreground status once per second, so the reset window can feel slightly jittery.

What permissions does the app need?

Usage Access to detect the foreground app, overlay permission to show reminders, and optional battery optimization exemption for reliability.

Is crash reporting on by default?

No. Crash reporting stays off unless you explicitly opt in.
